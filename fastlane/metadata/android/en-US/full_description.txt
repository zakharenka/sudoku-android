Sudoku, drawn by hand.

Take a break with a classic 9×9 Sudoku in a warm, hand-drawn style: paper texture, wobbly lines and a handwritten font. No rush, no noise — just you and the grid.

FEATURES
• Four difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert
• Every puzzle has a single solution
• Notes, hints, undo and erase
• Timer, mistake counter and pause
• Light, dark and system themes
• Nine languages: English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian, Ukrainian and Belarusian
• Game history with mini puzzle previews and one-tap resume
• Best results split by games with and without mistakes
• Animated handwritten digits and keyboard input
• Backup to a file and restore through the system file picker
• The current game is saved automatically and restored on pause
• Works offline
• No ads, no analytics, no account

Sudoku is a free game made by an independent developer. If you enjoy it, leave a review — it helps a lot.
